## What we're building

Your relationships live in DMs, and your DMs live in a different app for every platform. Inboxapp
brings them into one fast inbox with a CRM built in. Every person is one contact with a status,
tags, notes and reminders, so you always know where things stand and who to get back to. Teammates
work the same conversations from their own login, and nobody shares a password.

It works with **X**, **LinkedIn**, **WhatsApp** and **Telegram** today, with
[more platforms on the way](https://docs.inboxapp.com/help/platforms). Your agents can work in it
too: connect **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, **Codex** or **Cursor** to find who needs a reply, draft
messages and update contacts.

## Repositories

- **[inboxapp-ai-toolkit](https://github.com/InboxHQ/inboxapp-ai-toolkit)**: the Inboxapp plugin for Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, Cursor and Hermes, with skills to triage, reply and run your pipeline.
- **[inboxapp-sdk](https://github.com/InboxHQ/inboxapp-sdk)**: the official TypeScript SDK for the Inboxapp API, with Promise and Effect clients.
- **[n8n-nodes-inboxapp](https://github.com/InboxHQ/n8n-nodes-inboxapp)**: the n8n community node, to automate conversations and contacts from your workflows.

## Get started

- **App**: sign up at **[inboxapp.com](https://inboxapp.com)**, then [link your accounts](https://docs.inboxapp.com/get-started/set-up).
- **Agents**: `npx skills add InboxHQ/inboxapp-ai-toolkit`, then connect the [MCP server](https://docs.inboxapp.com/v2/mcp-server) at `https://mcp.inboxapp.com/mcp`.
- **SDK**: `bun add @inboxapp/sdk`, then send your first message with the [quick start](https://docs.inboxapp.com/v2/quickstart).
- **n8n**: install `@inboxapp/n8n-nodes-inboxapp` from the community nodes.
